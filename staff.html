About Carlos Vaz Pinto

The Portuguese coach leads Inter Kashi in 2026-27 and is named as head coach on the official ISL club page. Khel Now reports that he previously coached Sreenidi Deccan in the second division. His philosophy, in his own words before the East Bengal game: "we cannot change our idea of the game because of the opponent."

The ISL page lists no other coaching staff. Wikipedia's infobox also names Vaz Pinto; its staff table still shows the earlier Abhijit Mondal-era staff.
