Inter Kashi complete squad for ISL 2026-27

← Back to home
Squad · 11 OCT 2026

Inter Kashi started their 2026-27 campaign with a 22-man registered squad, 16 of them new signings, and no foreign players. The team is led by Portuguese coach Carlos Vaz Pinto, formerly of second-division Sreenidi Deccan.

Who is in the squad

Goalkeepers: Albino Gomes (India international), Shubham Dhas, Mohit Singh Dhami.
Defenders: Laldinliana Renthlei, Pratik Chaudhari, Gursimrat Singh Gill, Nishchal Chandan, Wayne Vaz, Leewan Castanha, Sajad Hussain Parray, Aritra Das, Tanush Pandey.
Midfielders: Hitesh Sharma, Jitendra Singh, Karthik Panicker.
Forwards: Sumeet Passi (captain), Manvir Singh, Rosenberg Gabriel, Sreekuttan VS, Aphaoba Singh, Ayush Chhikara, Thoi Singh.

The ISL's official squad list also includes midfielder Sourav Das, taking the total to 23.

Schedule

The club plays its first eight games at home, at Balewadi in Pune, before away trips to Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi and Bengaluru. See the fixtures page.
