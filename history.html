Club History

  • 29 Jun 2023
    Club announced. Carlos Santamarina named first head coach; Peter Hartley is the first signing.
  • 28 Oct 2023
    First official match: a 2-2 draw with Gokulam Kerala in the I-League.
  • 2023-24
    Debut season ends 4th in the I-League; Mario Barco top scorer with 12.
  • Jul 2024
    Antonio López Habas appointed head coach.
  • Nov 2024
    Ekana Stadium declared unfit, so home games move to Kalyani Stadium, West Bengal.
  • Jan 2025
    Third kit released as a tribute to the Prayagraj Maha Kumbh.
  • Apr 2025
    Title race ends in controversy: the AIFF Appeals Committee names Churchill Brothers champions, and Inter Kashi appeals to the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS).
  • 18 Jul 2025
    CAS declares Inter Kashi I-League 2024-25 champions, earning promotion to the ISL.
  • Oct-Nov 2025
    Super Cup group stage: one draw and two defeats (including 0-3 v FC Goa).
  • 14 Feb 2026
    First ISL match: 1-1 with FC Goa.
  • 28 Feb 2026
    First ISL win: 2-1 away at Kerala Blasters.
  • 21 May 2026
    2025-26 ends with a 1-2 defeat to East Bengal, a result that crowned East Bengal champions. Inter Kashi finish 10th with 13 points.
  • May 2026
    Abhijit Mondal takes over from López Habas (who is now East Bengal's head coach).
  • 2026-27
    Portuguese coach Carlos Vaz Pinto leads an all-Indian squad, playing home games in Pune.
  • 11 Oct 2026
    ISL 2026-27 opener: 0-2 loss to Kerala Blasters.
  • 17 Oct 2026
    1-1 draw with Odisha FC after Thoi Singh's 89th-minute equaliser.
Dates from the ISL site, Wikipedia and Khel Now. The exact date Carlos Vaz Pinto was appointed was not found in any source.
